Frequently asked

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 17:00 and 20:00, and check-out is by 10:00. Arriving outside those hours? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to arrange it.

How do I collect the keys?

Most homes have a smart lock or a lockbox with a code we send before arrival; for some we meet you in person. You'll get clear directions and everything you need the day before check-in.

Is a deposit required?

A refundable security deposit and ID are required at check-in under Spanish law. The deposit is returned after check-out, once the home is confirmed in good order (usually within a few days).

Do you register guests with the police?

Yes. Spanish law requires guest registration; we handle it digitally before or at arrival, so you're fully compliant with nothing to worry about.

Is there Wi-Fi and air conditioning?

Every home has fast Wi-Fi and air conditioning. The network name and password are in your welcome guide and displayed in the apartment.

Are pets allowed?

Some homes are pet-friendly under the owner's rules. Ask before booking and we'll match you with the right apartment.

How do I get support during my stay?

WhatsApp is fastest: a real, local person answers day or night at +34 666 64 72 45. You can also email info@domiastays.com.

Good to know

Getting here
Alicante-Elche airport (ALC) is about 45 minutes away. We can arrange a transfer on request.
House rules
Respect community quiet hours, no parties, and leave the home as you found it. Full rules are in each apartment.
Emergencies
112 for all emergencies. Non-urgent issue with the apartment? Message us and we'll sort it quickly.

Still have a question?

Tell us your dates and who's coming. We reply personally, usually within the hour.
