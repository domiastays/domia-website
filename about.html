About Domia Stays

A local agency with an international outlook, built on one idea: make every stay feel effortless.

Our story

Feel at home, anywhere

Domia Stays Group was born on the Costa Blanca out of a simple belief: a holiday home should feel effortless — for the guest who books it and the owner who trusts us with it. What began with a handful of apartments has grown into a curated portfolio across Benidorm, run by a local team who treat every home as their own.

We deliberately stay small enough to care. Every property is visited, styled, photographed and maintained by people who live here, so what a guest sees online is exactly what they walk into. Hospitality standards, real-estate expertise and a revenue-driven mindset — all in service of one promise: feel at home, anywhere.
